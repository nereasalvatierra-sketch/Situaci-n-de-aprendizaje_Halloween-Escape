6-7 Designing the Haunted House

Let's start organizing

In groups, you will design your own haunted house experience for students of 1st ESO. You will decide the scenes, characters, and script, and organise how everything will happen during the final performance. You will have two sessions to plan everything carefully.

You will decide:

-The different rooms or scenes of the haunted house
-The characters (ghosts, witches, visitors, etc.)
-The storyline of the experience
-The dialogues and interactions
-The visual design and atmosphere

Your final goal is to create an immersive experience for 1st ESO students using English as the main language of communication.

Use your creativity and make sure everyone in the group participates and contributes.

To promote organization, I encourage you to share a Padlet Board witn your group.

Useful expressions for your dialogues:


You must use English during the performance. Here are some helpful expressions:

👻 Entering the haunted house
Welcome to the haunted house… if you dare!
Don’t go in there… it’s dangerous!
Are you ready for the challenge?
 
😱 Giving instructions or warnings
You must find the key to escape!
Be careful, the ghost is watching you!
Follow the clues to survive!
