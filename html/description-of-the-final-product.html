Halloween Escape

Discover, Learn and Scare

Description of the final product

Main objectives

For the development of the final product of this learning situation, in which 2nd ESO students will design a haunted house experience in English for 1st ESO students during Halloween, digital tools such as Canva and Genially will be used.

Through these tools, students will be able to create visual and interactive materials related to the project, such as posters, presentations, breakout-style challenges or digital elements to decorate and organise the different scenes of the haunted house experience.

On the one hand, Canva stands out for being an intuitive and user-friendly tool, suitable for 2nd ESO students with an intermediate level of digital competence. In addition, it allows students to design attractive visual resources that encourage creativity, motivation and active participation.

On the other hand, Genially enables the creation of interactive content and gamified activities, such as games, challenges and quizzes, which perfectly fit the theme of the project and promote active and cooperative learning.

Both tools are accessible within the educational environment and encourage collaborative work, helping students develop not only their digital competence, but also their autonomy and creativity.
