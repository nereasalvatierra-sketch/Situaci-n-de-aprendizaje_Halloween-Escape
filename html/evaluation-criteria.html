Evaluation criteria

Assessment

To help you understand how you are doing, you will use a checklist and a rubric that will show what is expected in each part of the project. You will also reflect on your own learning and evaluate your teamwork.

Remember: making mistakes is part of learning. What matters most is your effort, your participation, and your willingness to communicate in English.
